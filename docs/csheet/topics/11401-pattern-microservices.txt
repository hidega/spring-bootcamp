Microservices
microservice
Microservice architecture is an architectural style that structures an application as a collection of 
services that are independently deployable, loosely coupled, organized around business capabilities, 
owned by a small team. The most important pattern categories are <i>Decomposition Patterns</i>, <i>Integration Patterns</i>,
<i>Database Patterns</i>, <i>Observability Patterns</i> and <i>Cross cutting concerns Patterns</i>.
